繁體中文

Petanque 3D 是一款 3D 法式滾球遊戲，規則依照 FIPJP 官方規則。你可以和電腦對戰、同一台裝置輪流對戰，或是線上對戰。

聯絡我們

遇到問題或有建議，請到 GitHub Issues 留言。回報問題時請附上：

  • 裝置型號和 iOS 版本
  • App 版本（App Store 頁面或 TestFlight 可以看到）
  • 發生了什麼事，以及你預期會發生什麼事

常見問題

怎麼投球？主選單的「新手教學」會一步步帶你練習站位、瞄準和投擲。

線上對戰連不上？請確認網路正常。伺服器閒置一段時間後會休眠，第一次連線可能要等大約一分鐘。

可以改慣用手或視角嗎？可以，在「設定」裡調整，改動會立刻生效。

English

Petanque 3D is a 3D pétanque game that follows the official FIPJP rules. Play against the computer, pass one device between players, or play online.

Contact us

For problems or suggestions, please open an issue on GitHub Issues. When you report a problem, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version (shown on the App Store page or in TestFlight)
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen

FAQ

How do I throw? Open Tutorial from the main menu. It walks you through standing, aiming and throwing.

I can't connect to online play. Check your network connection. The server sleeps when it has been idle, so the first connection can take about a minute.

Can I change handedness or the camera view? Yes. Change them in Settings. Changes take effect at once.
