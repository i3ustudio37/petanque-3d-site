繁體中文

Petanque 3D（以下稱「本 App」）是一款法式滾球遊戲。本頁說明本 App 處理哪些資料。簡短的結論是：本 App 不收集任何可以識別你的資料。

我們不收集的資料

  • 本 App 不需要帳號，不要求姓名、Email、電話或生日。
  • 本 App 不讀取位置、聯絡人、照片、相機或麥克風。
  • 本 App 沒有廣告、分析或追蹤工具，也不和第三方分享資料。

只存在你裝置上的資料

語言、慣用手、視角、音量等設定只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。刪除本 App 就會一併刪除這些設定。

線上對戰

使用線上對戰時，本 App 會用加密連線（wss）連到我們的遊戲伺服器。伺服器架設在 Render 平台（新加坡機房）。

  • 連線只傳送對戰需要的內容：房間代碼、投擲操作，以及斷線重連用的暫時憑證。
  • 房間和憑證只存在伺服器的記憶體裡。對戰結束或伺服器重新啟動後就會消失，不寫入任何資料庫或檔案。
  • 我們的伺服器程式不記錄你的 IP 位址。
  • Render 平台為了維運和安全，可能在它的系統日誌中短暫保留連線的 IP 位址。這部分依照 Render 的隱私權政策 處理，我們不會用它來識別你。

資料保存與刪除

我們沒有保存任何可以識別你的資料，所以沒有帳號資料需要刪除。裝置上的設定可以直接刪除本 App 來清除。如果你對資料處理有疑問，可以透過下方的聯絡方式提出。

兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡，不會向任何人收集個人資料，包括兒童。

政策變更

如果本政策有變更，我們會更新這一頁並修改上方的生效日期。

聯絡方式

請到 GitHub Issues 留言。

English

Petanque 3D ("the app") is a pétanque game. This page explains what data the app handles. In short: the app does not collect any data that identifies you.

Data we do not collect

  • The app has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email, phone number or birthday.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and shares no data with third parties.

Data that stays on your device

Settings such as language, handedness, camera view and volume are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app also deletes these settings.

Online play

When you play online, the app connects to our game server over an encrypted connection (wss). The server runs on the Render platform in Singapore.

  • The connection carries only what a match needs: the room code, your throws, and a temporary token for reconnecting after a dropped connection.
  • Rooms and tokens exist only in server memory. They disappear when the match ends or the server restarts, and are never written to a database or file.
  • Our server software does not log your IP address.
  • For operations and security, Render may briefly keep connection IP addresses in its system logs. Render handles this under the Render Privacy Policy. We do not use it to identify you.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no data that identifies you, so there is no account data to delete. To remove the settings on your device, delete the app. If you have questions about how data is handled, contact us using the link below.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
